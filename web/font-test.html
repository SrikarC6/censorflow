Dot-matrix display font

Every glyph in web/font5x7.js. Two sets: sans (default, 5x7) and serif (7x9). Sans is the default: at this dot size it is the clearer of the two, and the 7x9 serif was tried and dropped because its A and W were hard to read. Press FONT: SERIF to compare them. Gaps are 1 dot between letters and 2 between words. Uppercase-only: glyphFor() uppercases its input, so lowercase renders as uppercase.

Board controls — hover, press, and use the toggles

Drawn on the real board at grid coordinates with hit-testing in grid space. ANIMATE turns on the occasional background flip; WIPE runs the full board-wide dance; SOUND is off until you press it, because browsers only allow audio after a click. Everything here respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Each of these sits on an opaque plate sized to the text with a dot of margin, so the small glyphs have quiet ground and the rectangle reads as floating over the field. The outline does not affect layout, which is why the dots inside still land on the grid.
