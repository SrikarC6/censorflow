CensorFlow 5x7 flip-disc font

Every glyph in web/font5x7.js, at scale 1 and scale 2. Gaps are 1 dot between letters and 2 between words, counted in font pixels so they scale with the glyph. The set is uppercase-only: glyphFor() uppercases its input, so lowercase renders as uppercase. Characters with no glyph fall back to ? and are flagged in red below.

Board controls — hover and press a button

Drawn on the real board, at grid coordinates, with hit-testing in grid space. Hover and press swap the two colours. The reserved strip is at the very top of the board, so scroll to the top to reach it.
