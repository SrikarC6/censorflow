Dot-matrix display font

Every glyph in web/font5x7.js. Two sets: sans (default, 5x7) and serif (7x9). Sans is the default: at this dot size it is the clearer of the two, and the 7x9 serif was tried and dropped because its A and W were hard to read. Press FONT: SERIF to compare them. Gaps are 1 dot between letters and 2 between words. Uppercase-only: glyphFor() uppercases its input, so lowercase renders as uppercase. The board itself is bare black — dots are ink, never a backdrop. The sign along the very top scrolls right to left; it is the one animation the brief ruled out, added because a sentence wider than its sign has to scroll or truncate, and scrolling shows all of it.

Board controls — hover and press

Drawn on the real board at grid coordinates with hit-testing in grid space, and built from the same plate as the specimens below: opaque panel, one hairline border, a dot of air inside. The border is what carries meaning — green for a control that goes, red for one that stops, plain for neither. Hover or press and the panel lights in its own colour with the label knocked out of it. OFF is disabled: it keeps its plate and dims its label. SOUND is a toggle and stays clickable in both states; its border tells you which one you are in. The board is bare, so there is no wipe and nothing idle-flips.
