Dot-matrix display font

Every glyph in web/font5x7.js. Two sets: sans (default, 5x7) and serif (7x9). Sans is the default: at this dot size it is the clearer of the two, and the 7x9 serif was tried and dropped because its A and W were hard to read. Press FONT: SERIF to compare them. Gaps are 1 dot between letters and 2 between words. Uppercase-only: glyphFor() uppercases its input, so lowercase renders as uppercase. The board itself is bare black — dots are ink, never a backdrop.

Board controls — hover and press

Drawn on the real board at grid coordinates with hit-testing in grid space. A button is a sign: board-coloured inside, an amber frame at rest, and the whole thing swaps light and dark the moment you point at it or press it. OFF is disabled, so it keeps its shape and dims instead of vanishing.

Samples on floating plates — scale 4 / 3 / 2 / 1

Every specimen below sits on an opaque plate sized to the text with a dot of margin. The board behind is deliberately bare: an always-on grid of unlit dots was noise behind every label, so now the dots are only ever ink and a plate is what a label stands on. The outline does not affect layout, which is why the dots inside still land on the grid.
